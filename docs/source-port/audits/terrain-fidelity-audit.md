Adversarially-verified audit of the terrain collision and physics layer of the Kid Chameleon source-port. The probe geometry and tile decode are largely faithful: row/column arithmetic, span endpoints, the half-width tables, the disguised-ghost filter and the slope-nibble mapping all match the ROM. The failures cluster in how the native code consumes what those probes find. The ROM's shared horizontal cell scan (`sub_914A`/`sub_922C`) walks the whole hitbox span and tags `collision_type=colid_kidbelow` ($28) on any spike cell it crosses; the native floor/wall/ceiling paths collapse each scan to a single selected cell and drop that tag. So a spike above the head deals no damage, a spike masked by a taller solid deals no damage, and floor-spike contact becomes unconditional instant death rather than the ROM's helmet/HP/invuln-gated hit. Separately, Lion airborne charge now consumes `sub_36B3C`/`sub_36BD6` body-overlap and lead-column X-stop results plus the `sub_36C6A`/`sub_36CB8` vertical full-span scans, while the down-look camera pan was dead because the look accumulator was capped at exactly the value where the pan would begin.
