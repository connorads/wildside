# Boss Encounters Fidelity Audit

Adversarially-verified per-unit audit of the Kid Chameleon source-port boss subsystem. The dominant failure pattern is overwhelming and singular: the entire boss corpus is a faithful-but-dark shadow implementation. Almost every boss mechanic — the `sub_39846` waypoint movement engine, the per-head attack FSMs (`loc_37F30`/`loc_38012`/`loc_380F4`), the `sub_3A292` collision/HP/palette handler, the orbiting eye satellites, and the `sub_37708`/`sub_378C8` HeadyMetal breakup — is ported into `combat.rs` as an ASM-faithful, unit-tested helper carrying `#[cfg_attr(not(test), allow(dead_code))]` with zero production callers. The three multi-head bosses still collapse onto `EnemyArchetype::Tracker`, run a generic player-chasing `step_tracker` plus a fixed-clock `step_boss_fire` (`BOSS_FIRE_INTERVAL=180`), and use invented projectile velocities. HeadyMetal tracking now delegates to the ASM helper for its base velocity, but the broader composite/fire/breakup handler remains unwired. Boss-level flagpole collision suppression and the formerly wrong Shishkaboss heads 2/3 HP values are fixed. The net effect is that a player or TAS still observes materially wrong boss motion, firing cadence, projectile trajectories, and defeat sequences.
