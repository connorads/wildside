# Terrain Collision & Physics Fidelity Audit

Adversarially-verified audit of the terrain collision and physics layer of the Kid Chameleon source-port. The probe geometry and tile decode are largely faithful: row/column arithmetic, span endpoints, the half-width tables, the disguised-ghost filter and the slope-nibble mapping all match the ROM. The dominant failure pattern is in the *consumption* of what those probes find. The ROM's shared horizontal cell scan (`sub_914A`/`sub_922C`) walks the whole hitbox span and tags `collision_type=colid_kidbelow` ($28) on any spike cell it crosses; the native floor/wall/ceiling paths collapse each scan to a single selected cell and drop that tag entirely — so a spike above the head deals no damage, a spike masked by a taller solid deals no damage, and floor-spike contact is rewritten as unconditional instant death rather than the ROM's helmet/HP/invuln-gated hit. Alongside this, several ASM-faithful full-span scanners (`probe_floor_span`, `sub_36C6A`/`sub_36CB8`, `sub_36B3C`/`sub_36BD6`) are correctly ported but carry no production caller, and the down-look camera pan is dead because the look accumulator is capped at exactly the value at which the pan would begin.

## Confirmed gaps

### High severity

**Ceiling spike deals no damage; native treats a spike tile above the head as a plain solid ceiling** (`loc_91A6 / loc_9188 / sub_914A / Character_CheckCollision / Check_for_recent_damge`)
In `sub_914A`'s upward span scan, a Spikes ($7000) tile in the head-row branches to `loc_91A6`, which writes `collision_type(a3)=colid_kidbelow` ($28) before falling into the stop handler; the next move-state loop's `Character_CheckCollision` reads $28, does `subq.w #4,d7` and indexes `off_B640` entry 9 = `Check_for_recent_damge`, decrementing `Number_Hitpoints` (Just_received_damage-gated; last HP at helmetless Kid → death). The native live ceiling path `apply_ceiling_collision_inner` matches Spikes alongside `Solid | HiddenBlock` and only snaps `y_pos` and zeroes `y_velocity` exactly like a solid ceiling: it never sets `on_spikes`, never writes a `colid_kidbelow` equivalent, and queues no damage. Floor spikes are handled (every floor probe sets `on_spikes`), but the ceiling probe is the only spike sensor that drops the harm outright, so jumping head-first into a spike ceiling costs nothing in native where the ROM costs a hitpoint/helmet (or a life at 1 HP). Kind: missing, `crates/kid-core/src/movement.rs:6235` (probe match at `terrain.rs:2451`).

**Spike contact is unconditional instant death; the ROM routes it through the normal helmet/HP/invuln-gated damage path** (`loc_91A6 / loc_92B6 / Character_CheckCollision / off_B640 / Check_for_recent_damge / loc_B700 / loc_B786`)
In the ROM a spike ($7000) cell detected by the floor scan (`sub_914A` `loc_91A6`) or the wall/ceiling scan (`sub_922C` `loc_92B6`) only sets `collision_type=colid_kidbelow` ($28). Each move-state frame's `Character_CheckCollision` then routes $28 → `off_B640` entry 9 = `Check_for_recent_damge`: if `Just_received_damage` is set it returns (invulnerable), otherwise it decrements `Number_Hitpoints`, calling `Death` only when a helmetless Kid reaches 0 HP, else losing a helmet and granting invulnerability (`loc_B700`/`loc_B786`). The native instead treats any `on_spikes` floor contact as unconditional instant death: `combat.rs:10898-10899` forces `number_hitpoints=0` and calls `begin_death_windup()` with no helmet/HP/invuln check, even though the faithful `loc_B700` port `apply_damage` (`combat.rs:9200`) already exists and is the live damage path for enemies, projectiles and the murder wall. The native comment "contacting a spike cell is instant death" carries no `// DEVIATION` marker, and the regression `spike_death_ignores_hitpoints` pins the invented behaviour while citing no ASM label. A helmeted player (should lose a helmet), an extra-HP player (should lose 1 HP) and an already-invulnerable player (should take no damage) all die instantly. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/combat.rs:10895-10902`.

### Medium severity

**Spike under part of the hitbox is masked when a taller solid wins 'highest floor' selection** (`sub_914A / loc_91A6 / loc_91AC`)
`sub_914A` continues scanning the whole foot-row span after the first hit (`loc_91AC` falls into `loc_919E` → `dbf`), so every cell is inspected and any Spikes cell sets `collision_type=$28`. The native landing path resolves a single cell via `probe_floor_hitbox` "highest floor wins" (`select_higher_floor` keeps the smaller `floor_y_px`) and then sets `on_spikes` only from that one selected cell (`self.player.on_spikes = probe.cell.collision == Spikes`). When the hitbox straddles a Spikes cell and a same-height Solid cell at the foot row, native selects the Solid (taller) and reports `on_spikes=false`, so the player takes no spike damage where the ROM sets $28. The center short-circuit means a spike directly under center is not masked; the gap is gated on an off-center edge spike beside a same-height selected solid, realizable in normal play for forms whose half-width spans more than one tile column. The ceiling sibling path (`probe_ceiling`) is full-span and preserves Spikes, so the floor path is asymmetrically deficient. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/movement.rs:6762` (selector at `terrain.rs:1188`).

**Standing-still wall gate scans the full 31px column for Micromax/Juggernaut; `sub_922C` uses the 15px short body** (`sub_922C / loc_9288`)
`sub_922C` derives the head-row from `y_pos` and at `loc_9284-9290` skips the extra `subi.w #$10,d7` (so the scan spans only 15px) when `Current_Helmet` is Micromax or Juggernaut (or `MoveID_Crawling` / the special sprite). The live standing-still wall gate `should_hold_standing_still` passes the literal `PLAYER_HEIGHT` (31) to `probe_wall_column` for every form, scanning one tile row above the short body of these helmets. A wall tile present only in that extra upper row makes native keep facing/standing and suppress the start-walking transition (`loc_7820`) where the ROM, seeing no wall over the short body, begins walking. The moving/jump collision path correctly uses `form.ceiling_probe_height()` (15 for Micromax/Juggernaut) — the `sub_8F26` short-scan fix (#258) touched only that path, leaving the `loc_779C`/`loc_77E6` standing gate on the stale literal 31. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/movement.rs:4478` (height arg at `:4483`).

**`sub_36B3C`/`sub_36BD6` hitbox+lead-column scan is ASM-faithful but never wired into the live airborne charge path** (`sub_36B3C / sub_36BD6 / loc_36BA0 / loc_36C38 / loc_3E6AE / loc_3E7CA`)
`sub_36B3C` (right) / `sub_36BD6` (left) are the body-rectangle + lead-column horizontal scan at the top of the airborne charge loops (`Lion.asm` `loc_3E6AE`:642, `loc_3E7CA`:758, shared by Dragon/Ninja/Armadillo/Goat/Orca/Scorpion via the `loc_3E6AE`/`loc_3E7CA` dispatch). The native ports `horizontal_collision_scan_right_sub_36B3C`/`_left_sub_36BD6` → `horizontal_collision_scan` are internally faithful (degenerate-span `tst.w d2;bpl`, lead-col == `Level_width_blocks` boundary `seq d6`, left-edge col==0 → d6=1) but have zero production callers — every reference is inside `#[cfg(test)]`. The live charge step `step_lion_charge_movement_loc_3e11e` routes airborne motion to `step_dragon_walking_airborne`, which uses a single-column `dragon_walking_probe_wall` (matching `Solid|Spikes|SlopeUp` over 3 body rows, not bit-$4000 over the full inclusive span) plus a single-point `terrain::probe_floor`, computing neither the d5 hitbox mask nor the d6 lead-column mask. The substitute covers the common leading-wall stop, so this is a reachable-but-edge collision/motion divergence. Kind: helper-unwired, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:2296` / `:2312` (scan core at `:2205`); live path `crates/kid-core/src/enemies.rs:19267` → `:22127`.

**RESOLVED candidate — find_floor_below settle row-walk skips slopes like ASM `loc_ABF0`** (`loc_ABF0 / loc_ABA4 / loc_ABD4`)
`loc_ABF0` is a downward row-walk whose only stop test is `andi.w #$7000,d7; cmpi.w #$6000,d7; blt.s loc_ABF0` — it continues while the collision class is < 6, so it stops only on Solid ($6000) or Spikes ($7000) and walks straight past SlopeUp ($4000)/SlopeDown ($5000). Native `find_floor_below` now uses that Solid/Spikes-only class check instead of `cell.is_solid_floor()`, so a slope in the scan column is skipped until the next Solid/Spikes row. Regression `find_floor_below_loc_abf0_skips_slopes_until_solid_or_spikes` pins the `$4000/$5000` skip. The adjacent aligned-start-row issue remains open below; this fix only changes the stop class. Kind: fixed-candidate, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs`.

### Low severity

**Live floor-snap probe samples only 3 columns; `sub_914A` scans the full hitbox span (the faithful span helper is unwired)** (`sub_914A / loc_9188 / loc_919E`)
`sub_914A` seeds the scan from `(x_pos-FA78)>>4` to `(x_pos+FA78)>>4` and scans every cell via `dbf d5,loc_9188`. The live landing path `apply_floor_collision` → `probe_floor_hitbox` samples only three columns (center, center±half_width) and picks the highest. The faithful full-span scanner `probe_floor_span` exists but is called only at level-entry settling and tests, never per frame. For the widest form (Juggernaut, FA78=$17=23) the span reaches 4 cells — e.g. `x_pos=31` gives sampled cols {0,1,3}, leaving col 2 (px 32-47) unscanned, so a single-column interior solid platform under the hitbox is missed on the landing frame while `sub_914A` would land on it. Narrower forms (hw≤10) cover their ≤3-cell span fully and are unaffected, so this is a Juggernaut-only narrow geometric edge. Kind: helper-unwired, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:1175` / span helper `:1210`, landing path `movement.rs:6501`.

**Side-wall spike hit does not set `collision_type=colid_kidbelow` ($28)** (`loc_92B6 / loc_9294`)
In `sub_922C` a Spikes ($7000) cell in the leading column is treated as a wall hit and first writes `collision_type(a3)=colid_kidbelow` ($28) at `loc_92B6` before the hit handler. The native `probe_wall_column` correctly returns `hit:true` for a Spikes cell (the wall-stop is faithful) but never writes the $28 tag. No direct $28 damage consumer was located for the side-wall case in the disasm (`colid_hurt=$1C` is the damage code), so this is a classification/advisory omission rather than a damage outcome. Kind: missing, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:1394`, live caller `movement.rs:5979`.

**`vertical_collision_scan_down/up` (`sub_36C6A`/`sub_36CB8`) helpers are faithful but have no production caller** (`sub_36C6A / sub_36CB8 / loc_36CAA / loc_36D00 / loc_3E732 / loc_3E6F6 / loc_3E84E / loc_3E812`)
The terrain helpers are arithmetically faithful (correct row math, inclusive DBF span, bit-$4000 kind set), but every call site is inside `#[test]`. The only ROM caller of these two subs is the Lion (the sole file in `code/enemy/` to reference them; `kid.asm` references neither). The live Lion path `step_lion_charge_movement_loc_3e11e` never runs the ASM ballistic Y-motion: instead of `sub_36C6A`'s full-row OR scan across x±$48(a5) (=±20px) it substitutes `probe_floor_hitbox`/`probe_floor` (`ENEMY_PROBE_HALF_WIDTH=8`, a 3-point ±8px edge probe). The observable live consequence — Lion charge being purely horizontal with no y_vel/gravity/floor-snap — is already a confirmed prior gap (`enemy-fidelity-audit.md:192-193`); this entry records the matching unwired terrain-domain half. Kind: helper-unwired, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:2326` / `:2362` / `:2380`, live Lion path `enemies.rs:19267`.

**Lead-column (d6) gating of the airborne charge X-snap replaced by a leading-edge wall probe** (`loc_36BA0 / loc_36C38 / loc_3E6AE / loc_3E7CA`)
After the body scan, the ASM uses the d6 lead-column result together with a tile-boundary displacement test (`d3 = (-(x+$48))&$F`, `cmp d1,d3 / bcc`; left mirrors via `(x-$48)&$F`) to decide the X commit, snapping x to the tile edge and clearing x_vel only when the move crosses the boundary and d6 (lead column blocked, including the boundary case) is non-zero. The faithful commit helpers `lion_left_charge_x_commit_loc_3e6f0` and `lion_charge_collision_x_commit_loc_3e80c` exist but are test-only; the live `step_dragon_walking_airborne` instead snaps via `dragon_walking_wall_snap_x` on a 3-row `probe_wall` hit, so the precise lead-column + `andi #$F` boundary alignment of the charge stop differs. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/enemies.rs:1925` / `:2204`, live path `enemies.rs:22127`.

**Live left/right turn dispatch ignores `sub_36972`/`sub_36A58` `flags_d7` bit 1 (wall) and bit 2 (hurt); turning delegated to generic `probe_wall`/`probe_floor`** (`sub_36972 / sub_36A58 / loc_369DE / loc_36ABE`)
The ROM walker dispatch tests three d7 bits from the scan: bit 2 (`btst #2,d7; bne loc_3E94E` hurt/overlap), bit 1 (wall in lead column), bit 0 (floor edge). Native only partially consumes them — the rightward plan reads bits 0 and 1, bit 2 is never read on either side, and the leftward side reads no d7 bits — substituting a generic `probe_wall` call and a `probe_floor` look-ahead. Because these substitutes are single-column / hitbox-edge probes rather than the OR-reduced lead-column+following-row masks the scan computes, the turn timing/condition can diverge in mixed-cell cases ($5000 right / $4000 left following-lead nibble) that the scan distinguishes but the substitute does not. No specific divergent frame is proven. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/enemies.rs:16312` / `:16428` / `:16560`.

**Negative-coordinate guard returns Empty instead of reading the padded rows / unsigned-X wrap** (`sub_B55C / loc_331D2 / loc_331F6 / loc_33286`)
`sub_B55C`/`loc_331D2` compute the row via `asr.w #4` (signed) on `y_pos+offset` and the column via `lsr.w #4` (unsigned) on `x_pos+offset`, then index the `$FFFF4A04` row-pointer table, which is allocated with 2 valid rows above the top and 8 below. Native short-circuits `if sample_x < 0 || sample_y < 0 { return 0 }` and clamps cell coords with `.max(0)`, so for a probe 1-2 cells above the level top the ROM reads a real (normally empty) padded row while native returns Empty. In normal play these padded rows are empty so both yield Empty, but the implementations differ at the edge. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:1477` / `:1578` / `:1634`.

**`loc_3330E` Y coordinate uses `lsr` (unsigned) in ASM but native delegates to the `asr`-equivalent path** (`loc_3330E`)
`loc_3330E` differs from `sub_B55C`/`loc_331D2` in two ways: the X/Y offsets are in swapped registers and the Y coordinate is shifted with `lsr.w #4,d7` (unsigned) rather than `asr.w #4` (signed). The native `relative_collision_mask_loc_3330E` forwards to `relative_collision_mask_loc_331D2`, which uses signed `px >> 4` for Y plus the negative-Y guard. The behaviours diverge only for negative `y_pos+offset`; the Drips surface-crawler callers use ±5 offsets, so a crawler within 5px of the level top would hit the difference, which the negative guard masks to Empty. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:1547`, callers `projectiles.rs:1119` / `:1171`.

**find_floor_below tests the aligned start row that ASM `loc_ABF0` skips before its first read** (`loc_ABF0`)
In the ROM the search Y is `d6 = (y_pos-$14) & $FFF0`, and `loc_ABF0` executes `add.w (Level_width_tiles),a4` and `addi.w #$10,d6` before its first cell read, so the first tested cell is one row below the aligned start. Native `find_floor_below` loops `for row in start_row..grid.height_cells` starting at `start_row = pixel_to_cell((y-$14)&!0xF)` inclusive, testing the aligned start row first. If that aligned row is itself Solid/Spikes, native returns it while the ROM would have skipped it and continued to the next solid row below, yielding a different settle Y. The pull-up caller partially masks this via the head-probe gates at y-20/y-36, but the settle caller (`level.rs:18816`) has no such gate. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/terrain.rs:1157`.

**Missing `loc_72A8` `subi.w #1,($FFFFFB58)` decrement when the look-down offset overshoots `max_y`** (`loc_728C / loc_72A8`)
At `loc_728C`, after the offset-shifted Y target is re-clamped against `max_y` (d1), the ASM additionally decrements the look accumulator (`cmp.w d1,d0 / bcs.s loc_72A8 / move.w d1,d0 / subi.w #1,($FFFFFB58).w`), self-limiting it once the panned target hits the bottom bound. The native applies the offset and re-clamps via `update_camera_axis` (`.min(max_pos)`), reproducing the target clamp but never performing the accumulator pull-back; the two per-frame pull-backs that are ported (`loc_726C` -2, `loc_727E` -1) are wired but this third overshoot brake is absent. It has no currently observable effect because the separate `.min(30)` accumulator cap (see below) keeps the pan from ever growing, so the overshoot precondition is unreachable today. Kind: missing, `crates/kid-core/src/movement.rs:7084`, target clamp `physics.rs:894`, ported pull-backs `lib.rs:3125`.

**Native adds an extra [0,max_pos] clamp to the camera result not present in ASM `loc_724E`/`loc_72E2`** (`loc_724E / loc_72E2`)
After the per-frame velocity (clamped to ±8), the ASM simply does `add.w d2,(Camera_X_pos)` / `add.w d3,(Camera_Y_pos)` with no clamp of the resulting camera position to the level bounds — it relies on the earlier target clamp plus the ±8 velocity limit. Native `update_camera_axis` applies an additional `(camera + velocity).max(0).min(max_pos)` and `update_camera_follow` re-applies `.max(0)`. In normal play this is unobservable because the velocity edge branches and ±8 cap keep the camera in range, but it diverges from the spec and could differ in pathological cases (negative `max_pos`, where the ASM unsigned compare differs from native signed `.min()`). The native comment acknowledges the extra clamp. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/physics.rs:927`, `movement.rs:7104`.

**Down-look camera pan dead: `.min(30)` cap on `$FFFFFB58` makes the look-down shift always 0** (`loc_728C / loc_7772 / loc_8C70 / loc_8218`)
The down-look accumulator `$FFFFFB58` is incremented by 1 each grounded frame while Down is held, with no upper cap in the ASM (`addq.w #1` at three sites). The camera Y target shift at `loc_728C` is `shift = offset - $1E (30)`, applied once `offset >= $1E`, so after 30 frames of holding Down the ROM begins panning the view down by `(offset-30)` pixels, growing while held. Native caps the accumulator at exactly 30 (`(self.y_look_offset + 1).min(30)`) and applies `player_y_px += offset - 0x1E` only when `offset >= 0x1E`; with the cap at exactly 30 the shift is always 0, so the look-down pan never occurs. The shift function itself is faithful (a direct-offset test gets a 10px shift); the defect is solely the upstream cap, which the refuter downgraded from high to medium given the narrow framing-only impact, and which sits at low here against the unit's own gap-2 interaction. The supposed pinning test asserts `incr(29)==30, incr(30)==30` with comment "ASM: capped at 30", codifying a value the ASM does not have. Kind: deviating, `crates/kid-core/src/lib.rs:3569`, shift apply `movement.rs:7090`.
